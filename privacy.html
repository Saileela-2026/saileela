Privacy Policy

How we collect, use and protect your information.

Last updated: August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Saileela ("we", "us"), operated by Sai Leela Devotainment Private Limited, collects and handles your information when you use our website and place orders. By using our site, you agree to this policy.

Information we collect

  • Order & delivery details — your name, mobile number, delivery address (with landmark, city, state and PIN code) and, where provided, email.
  • Payment information — payments are processed by our payment gateway (Razorpay). We do not store your full card, UPI or bank details on our servers; these are handled securely by the gateway.
  • Usage information — basic technical data such as browser type and pages visited, used to keep the site working and improve it.

How we use your information

  • To process, pack, ship and deliver your orders and provide order support.
  • To communicate with you about your order (for example, on WhatsApp, phone or email).
  • To handle returns, refunds and cancellations, and to prevent fraud.
  • To improve our products, service and website.

Sharing your information

We share information only as needed to run the store:

  • Courier & logistics partners (such as our shipping aggregator and delivery couriers) — to deliver your order and provide tracking.
  • Payment gateway (Razorpay) — to process your payment securely.
  • Legal requirements — where required by law or to protect our rights.

We do not sell your personal information.

Cookies & third-party tools

Our site may use cookies and third-party services for language translation, video/streaming and basic analytics. These help the site function and remember your preferences. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

Data security & retention

We take reasonable measures to protect your information and retain it only as long as needed for orders, support, and legal or accounting requirements.

Your rights

You may ask us to access, correct or delete your personal information, subject to legal and record-keeping obligations, by contacting us below.

Children

Our store is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page.
