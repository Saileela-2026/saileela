Cancellation Policy

Changed your mind? Here's how cancellation works.

Last updated: August 2026

This policy explains when and how a Saileela order can be cancelled.

Cancelling your order

You may cancel your order any time before it is dispatched. To cancel, contact us on WhatsApp or email (below) with your order number as early as possible.

After dispatch

Once an order has been handed to the courier, it can no longer be cancelled. If you no longer want it, you may choose to refuse delivery or follow our Refund & Return Policy after it arrives (subject to the non-returnable items listed there).

Refund on cancellation

For prepaid orders cancelled before dispatch, a full refund is processed to your original payment method, usually within 5–7 business days. Cash on Delivery orders cancelled before dispatch carry no charge.

Cancellation by us

Occasionally we may need to cancel an order — for example, if an item is out of stock, if there is a pricing or listing error, or if the delivery address is not serviceable. In such cases we will inform you and refund any amount paid in full.
