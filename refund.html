Refund & Return Policy

Our promise if something isn't right.

Last updated: August 2026

We want you to be happy with every order. If something is not right, this policy explains how returns, replacements and refunds work.

Return window

You may request a return within 7 days of delivery, provided the item is unused, in its original condition and original packaging, with all tags and accessories intact.

Items that cannot be returned

For hygiene, safety and the sacred nature of certain products, the following are non-returnable unless they arrive damaged or defective:

  • Consumable pooja items — incense, dhoop, chandan, kumkum, tika and similar samagri.
  • Edible or prasad items.
  • Personalised or made-to-order items.
  • Products returned without original packaging, or that show signs of use.

Damaged, defective or wrong item

If your order arrives damaged, defective or incorrect, please contact us within 48 hours of delivery with photos of the item and packaging. We will arrange a replacement or a full refund at no extra cost to you.

How to request a return

Contact us on WhatsApp or email (below) with your order number and reason. Once approved, we will guide you through return pickup or dispatch.

Refunds

  • Approved refunds for prepaid orders are processed to your original payment method via our payment gateway, usually within 5–7 business days of us receiving and inspecting the returned item.
  • For Cash on Delivery orders, refunds are made to your bank account or UPI, which we will collect securely.
  • Shipping charges are non-refundable unless the return is due to our error (damaged, defective or wrong item).

Replacements & exchanges

Where you prefer a replacement of the same item (subject to availability), we are happy to arrange it instead of a refund.
