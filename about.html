From a channel to your doorstep

Devotion you can hold in your hands

Saileela began as a devotional channel that lakhs of Sai devotees welcomed into their homes. Over the years, one request came again and again — "can you send us genuine things from Shirdi?"

The store is our answer. Run by devotees, based in Shirdi, bringing you authentic idols, books, frames and gifting — with the same trust the Saileela name has always carried.

  • Handpicked range — chosen by people who understand devotees.
  • Honest pricing — premium quality, without the tourist mark-up.
  • Real support — devotees on WhatsApp who actually reply.
