Our Story

॥ श्रद्धा • सबुरी • समर्पण ॥

Some channels are created to be watched.
Saileela TV was created to be felt.

Our beginning

Born in Shirdi in 2018, during the historic centenary year of Shri Sai Baba's Mahasamadhi, Saileela TV began as an offering of devotion — from Sai bhakts, for Sai bhakts. Its purpose was simple yet profound: to take the spirit of Shirdi beyond its physical boundaries and bring Sai Baba's presence, teachings and stories closer to devotees wherever they may be.

At the heart of Saileela are the timeless values associated with Baba — Shraddha, Saburi and the universal message of "Sabka Malik Ek". Through devotion expressed as stories, music, lived experiences and darshan, we seek to keep these values relevant not only in prayer, but in everyday life.

From the sacred rhythm of Sai Aarti and live darshan from Shirdi to stories from Baba's life, devotional music and heartfelt experiences of Sai devotees, Saileela brings together bhakti and meaningful storytelling. Our programmes are created to inform, inspire and, above all, deepen the devotee's connection with Baba. Saileela's present digital presence continues to feature live darshan, Aarti and Sai-focused programming, while its programme archive includes formats centred on real devotee experiences and the life and teachings of Sai Baba.

Our Philosophy

To bridge the distance to Shirdi

Life moves faster today, but the human need for faith, peace and inner strength has not changed. For countless Sai devotees, Shirdi is more than a destination — it is a feeling of surrender, reassurance and belonging. Yet distance, work, health, family responsibilities and everyday life can make a visit to Shirdi impossible whenever the heart wishes to be there.

Saileela was created to bridge that distance.

Through television and digital platforms, our endeavour is to keep devotees connected with Shirdi and with Baba's message — wherever life may take them. Today, Saileela can be experienced through its digital presence and services including YouTube, JioTV and ShemarooMe, alongside the channel's own online platform.

Sai Baba's own way of teaching gives this mission deeper meaning. The Shree Saibaba Sansthan Trust records that Baba rarely relied on formal lectures; instead, he taught through parables, experience and the example of his own life. Saileela carries that spirit into modern media — allowing a story, a bhajan, a devotee's experience or a moment of darshan to become a reminder of faith.

The spirit of our seva

These three words define the spirit in which Saileela serves its audience. "Shraddha" and "Saburi" are central to the teachings attributed to Sai Baba by the official Sansthan, while "Shraddha · Saburi · Samarpan" forms Saileela's present-day brand expression.

We believe Baba's message belongs not to one community, language or geography, but to humanity. His message of "Sabka Malik Ek", together with his emphasis on equality and harmony across faiths, remains as meaningful in today's divided and fast-moving world as ever. That is why Saileela is not merely about watching devotion. It is about living it.

Our Journey Continues

What began in Shirdi during the Sai Samadhi Centenary Year has grown beyond the boundaries of conventional television. Saileela today reaches devotees through live and digital viewing, while its website has expanded the experience to include Sai Aarti, Sai Darbar, live darshan and devotional offerings intended to bring a sense of Shirdi into the home.

Technology may change. Screens may change. The way people consume content may change.

The purpose does not.

  • To bring Shirdi closer.
  • To keep Baba's stories alive.
  • To share his message with generations to come.

And to create a space where every Sai devotee can pause, reconnect and remember —
