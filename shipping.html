Shipping Policy

How and when your order reaches you, straight from Shirdi.

Last updated: August 2026

Every Saileela order is packed with care and dispatched from Shirdi. This policy explains our shipping timelines and charges.

Order processing & dispatch

Orders are usually processed and dispatched within 1–3 business days of confirmation. Orders placed on Sundays or public holidays are processed on the next working day. During festivals and high-demand periods, dispatch may take a little longer.

Delivery timeline

Once dispatched, delivery typically takes 3–8 business days depending on your location, with metro cities generally faster than remote areas. Delivery timelines are estimates provided by our courier partners and are not guaranteed.

Shipping charges

  • Free shipping on all prepaid orders above ₹999.
  • A flat shipping fee of ₹60 applies to orders below ₹999.
  • Cash on Delivery (COD) may carry a small additional handling fee, shown at checkout where applicable.

Order tracking

Once your order ships, we will share tracking details over WhatsApp, phone or email so you can follow your parcel to your doorstep.

Delivery areas

We deliver across India through trusted courier partners. Serviceability for a particular PIN code is confirmed at the time of dispatch; if a location is not serviceable, we will contact you.

Delays & incorrect addresses

We are not responsible for delays caused by couriers, weather, strikes or other events beyond our control. Please provide a complete and correct address with landmark and PIN code — orders returned due to an incorrect address or repeated failed delivery may attract re-shipping charges.
